How to read this

Current AQI is computed in the bridge with the CPCB method (max sub-index of rolling means) over the simulated readings received so far. Next-step AQI and pollutant forecasts come from the trained models (Delhi CPCB site_113, 2023–2024). Each reading is treated as one hourly step, so the models need 48 readings (~8 minutes at 10 s uploads) before every lag feature is filled from real history; until then the status shows warming up. Benzene, toluene, xylene, NO and NOx are not simulated and are filled with training medians. Temperature, humidity and MQ-2 are not model inputs.

Data is held in memory on the bridge (last 100 readings) and resets when the Render service restarts.
